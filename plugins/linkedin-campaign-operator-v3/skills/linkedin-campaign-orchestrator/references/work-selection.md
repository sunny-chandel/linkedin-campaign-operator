# Work selection

Run a state audit before choosing work and again after each completed task.

## Selection rules

Prefer required recovery, genuine inbound evidence, due publication work, scheduled measurement, incomplete content stages, current engagement planning, opportunity refresh, inventory replenishment, and learning in that order. Choose a different useful task after two consecutive non-urgent tasks of the same type.

Use campaign settings for pacing, inventory, regional coverage, cooldowns, and quality thresholds. A target is a planning guide, not permission to lower quality, repeat activity, or exceed a configured limit.

One ready item is enough to proceed. Do not wait for a large batch when the current item is complete and eligible. Do not create filler work when no quality candidate exists.

## Continuation

When useful work remains, save the current result and choose the next task. When nothing useful is ready, record:

- the evidence checked;
- unfinished-work count;
- the next likely opportunity;
- the next evaluation time or event;
- the saved task to resume.

Maintain one continuation schedule for the campaign. Update the existing schedule rather than creating another one.

## Recovery

An unavailable app, account session, design tool, upload route, or connected service preserves the current checkpoint. Continue unaffected work, verify the repaired capability, and resume the same saved task. Never repeat an account request while its earlier outcome is unclear.
