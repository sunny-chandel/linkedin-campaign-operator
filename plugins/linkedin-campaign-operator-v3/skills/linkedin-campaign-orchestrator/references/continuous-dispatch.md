# Continuous adaptive dispatch v6

Run audit, rolling-output evaluation, and recorded dispatch at every wake and after every task. Leases, checkpoints, idempotency keys, and canonical external evidence make restart recovery deterministic.

## Rolling accounting

Count confirmed non-direct-inbound interactions in the immediately preceding 24 hours. Target 160 and cap at 200. Genuine direct inbound is logged separately outside the cap. Checkpoint pace at 40, 80, 120, and 160 across the campaign-local day, but never substitute those projections for canonical evidence.

Count unique verified publications in the immediately preceding 24 hours. Target six and cap at eight. Enforce at least 120 minutes between publications. Keep six validated normal packages available and replenish one after every publication.

## Selection

Priority is runtime repair, direct inbound, publication debt, mandatory recovery, canonical engagement execution, opportunity discovery, portfolio replenishment, scheduled analytics, then learning and investigation. One eligible candidate creates a one-action burst. Candidate execution always outranks another search.

Maintain at least 40 executable opportunities. Rotate the eight configured sources with 70/20/10 allocation. Backoff is per source. A low-yield pass switches sources immediately while debt remains and never creates generic reconciliation work.

Recovery tiers are 65/3,000/72, 60/2,000/48, and 55/1,000/24. Never go below intensive. Keep two proactive interactions per person per seven days. Outbound DMs require an existing connection and prior-interaction evidence.

## Publishing

The six normal slots bootstrap as two India, two US, one UK/EU, and one APAC. Evidence later selects four core and two exploration slots while India and US retain one each. Require four pillars, three format treatments, and no consecutive topic, angle, or format repeat. Up to two recovery posts are allowed, one unpublished recovery package at a time.

Schedule analytics at 30 minutes, 2 hours, 6 hours, and 24 hours. Completion requires snapshot, learning, experiment or no-change decision, and next trigger.

## Continuation and repair

A wait is valid only when no executable work remains. Record evidence, unfinished count, predicted opportunity, wake time, and trigger. Reuse and renew one campaign-deduplicated automation before any host expiry until target completion or owner stop.

Capability failures preserve the checkpoint, verify external outcome, route through runtime repair, continue unaffected work, verify recovery, and resume the original lease. Never duplicate an ambiguous external action.
