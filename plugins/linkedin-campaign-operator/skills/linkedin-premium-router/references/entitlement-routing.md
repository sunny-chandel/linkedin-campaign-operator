# Premium entitlement routing

Product features change. Confirm the exact tier and current official documentation before use.

## Typical mappings

- Premium Career: viewer trends, profile insights, learning resources, and relevant job features.
- Premium Business: company and viewer insights, search, featured profile content, custom buttons, Service Page features, and entitled InMail.
- Premium All-in-One: prospect suggestions, filters, insights, marketing tools, Company Page features, and included credits.
- Sales Navigator: exact Core, Advanced, or Advanced Plus tier; saved leads and accounts, searches, alerts, filters, relationship paths, intent signals, Smart Links, and connected CRM capabilities.
- Recruiter or Recruiter Lite: candidate search, projects, alerts, and hiring communication.
- Premium Company Page: visitor analytics, CTA, testimonials, credibility highlights, covers, services, and native invitations available to the connected Page role.
- LinkedIn Learning: background knowledge only; verify public factual claims independently.

## Setup record

For each enabled feature record:

- product and tier;
- account, Page, and user role;
- objective supported;
- setup steps and prerequisites;
- persistent settings changed;
- usage limit;
- metric and fallback;
- reversal steps.
