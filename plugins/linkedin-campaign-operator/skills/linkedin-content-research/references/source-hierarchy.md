# Source hierarchy and research brief

## Priority

1. Current official LinkedIn Help, engineering, release, and product documentation.
2. Primary technical or scientific evidence: papers, original repositories, changelogs, datasets, and direct announcements.
3. Reproducible independent studies that disclose dates, sample size, methodology, exclusions, and metrics.
4. Credible practitioner observations, used only to generate hypotheses.

Reject anonymous hacks, circular citations, uncited statistics, and hidden-method studies.

## Confidence

- `confirmed`: current official source or repeated strong campaign evidence.
- `high`: multiple strong independent sources and matching observations.
- `moderate`: plausible but limited; controlled testing required.
- `speculative`: anecdotal, contradictory, or untested.
