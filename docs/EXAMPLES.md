# Examples

## Solo founder

```text
Research two current topics at the intersection of AI agents and developer tools. Prepare one practical post and one opinionated post for my next content day.
```

## Technical professional

```text
Review my recent LinkedIn posts, identify the strongest content pillar at equal post age, and update the next two publication packages.
```

## Consultant

```text
Build an engagement queue around data platform leaders. Prioritize posts where a specific, useful reply can start a real conversation.
```

## Campaign operator

```text
Audit the campaign, recover any incomplete analytics stage, refresh the adaptive reserve, and execute the highest-priority ready work.
```

## Content-only mode

```text
Use the research and production skills only. Give me two source-backed LinkedIn packages and do not publish them.
```
