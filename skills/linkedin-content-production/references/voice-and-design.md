# Voice and design system

## Tier 1: visual assets

Strong, polished, well-structured, readable on mobile, and faithful to verified claims.

Before export, verify:

- clear hierarchy and limited copy;
- sufficient contrast;
- legible minimum type size;
- information is not conveyed through color alone;
- sources and required attribution are present;
- factual and visual accuracy;
- accurate alt text can be written.

## Tier 2: flagship captions

- Natural, human-written voice.
- Correct grammar; no deliberate mistakes.
- No em dash or en dash.
- Use real citations when the argument depends on factual claims.
- End with one direct question.
- Zero to three relevant hashtags.

## Tier 3: comments, DMs, and replies

- One to four short lines; a one-word response is allowed.
- Deliberately imperfect loose grammar and inconsistent capitalization.
- Always adversarial: challenge the other person's view or a common assumption.
- No em dash or en dash.
- Fact-check every claim regardless of length.

## Claude Design workflow

1. Re-check current Claude Design capabilities and limitations.
2. Create or open the intended project.
3. Supply the research brief, audience, dimensions, design system, and references.
4. Generate a first direction, then refine structure and hierarchy.
5. Use targeted comments for component changes and chat for structural revisions.
6. Verify the canvas visually before export.
7. Export the required format and confirm the resulting file opens correctly.
